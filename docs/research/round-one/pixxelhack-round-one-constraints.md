# PixxelHack 2.0 Round 1 constraints

Research date: 2 August 2026, IST

## Confirmed

The organizer's Unstop listing confirms:

- Round 1 is **Ideation & Proposal Submission**.
- Round 1 runs from **2 August 2026, 12:00 PM IST** to **3 August 2026, 11:59 AM IST**.
- Each team must choose one official problem statement and submit a presentation based on the supplied presentation template.
- Round 1 does not require a finished product. It tests the plan, creativity, and the chance of building a useful and engaging web app.
- Round 1 judging covers:
  - Problem Understanding
  - Innovation & Creativity
  - Technical Feasibility
  - Solution Design
  - Presentation Quality
- The wider brief asks for a user-centred web solution with strong usability, responsive design, accessibility, and an engaging experience.
- The event runs online.
- Teams may choose any language, framework, stack, or cloud service. The solution must be web-based.

The supplied problem-statement image confirms that Kairo must cover tasks, timetable, deadlines, notes, a Pomodoro timer, dark and light modes, and responsive use.
